About WhenWillIDie

Life expectancy calculator for entertainment

Explore a playful calendar date from adult age and a published population life table. WhenWillIDie is entertainment, never a prediction of your actual death.

What this site does

Import only demographic details, derive adult age from the source birth date, and explicitly reveal an entertainment date calculated from published population life expectancy. No medical diagnoses or individual mortality prediction.

A focused product

WhenWillIDie focuses on life expectancy calculator for entertainment. Its own interface, public documentation and source repository support the product’s continued development.

WhenWillIDie uses FinchNode only for its authorized demographics handoff. The entertainment calculation is separate code based on a published population life table; FinchNode does not provide the table or make a personal longevity prediction.

Current availability

Live record connections are awaiting production activation. Public guides are available now; deployment alone does not establish a completed patient connection.

Public information and private records

Guide search runs in your browser and covers public explanatory pages only. It does not access a patient session, index health records or send search terms to an analytics service. Opening the homepage or an article never starts a record import.

Read the privacy and data-handling notice before connecting. Medical questions belong with your care team; this site documents software behavior.

Source code and corrections

Inspect the public WhenWillIDie repository for implementation, license, setup and tests. For privacy questions or factual corrections, contact software@council.health. Please do not include private health records in a public GitHub issue.
